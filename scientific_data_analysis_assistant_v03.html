8. Interpretation and assumptions

Add data to generate interpretation notes.
Check before interpreting: independent experimental units; approximately normal model residuals; similar within-group variances; correct identification of biological versus technical replicates; and a design appropriate for one-way ANOVA.

Formula and validation notes

  • Group mean = Σx/n; sample variance = Σ(x − mean)²/(n − 1); SE = SD/√n.
  • One-way ANOVA: SSbetween = Σ nᵢ(meanᵢ − grand mean)²; SSwithin = ΣΣ(xᵢⱼ − meanᵢ)²; F = MSbetween/MSwithin.
  • Tukey–Kramer: q = |meanᵢ − meanⱼ| / √[MSE(1/2)(1/nᵢ + 1/nⱼ)]; adjusted p-values use the studentized-range distribution.
  • Not supported in this prototype: Welch ANOVA, nonparametric tests, repeated-measures or blocked designs, mixed models, formal Shapiro–Wilk normality testing, missing-data imputation, or native .xlsx import.
